Clear expectations from the start

WebAudX is a fast clarity review, not a full technical audit.

✓

What WebAudX checks

  • ✓ Connection & loading: Confirms the page loads over a secure connection.
  • ✓ Clarity & structure: Inspects title, search summary, and headings.
  • ✓ Readable content: Measures readable words and image descriptions.
  • ✓ Navigation & links: Checks menus, internal links, and samples broken links.
  • ✓ Clear next steps: Looks for action buttons and ways to contact the website.
  • ✓ Mobile display setting: Checks whether the page sizes properly on phones.
✕

What WebAudX does not do

  • ✕ No script execution: We read the page as delivered and do not run page scripts.
  • ✕ No forms or logins: We do not submit forms, log in, or interact with page elements.
  • ✕ No arbitrary scores: We provide findings and practical next steps, not letter grades or scores.
  • ✕ No multi-page crawl: Reviews focus strictly on the single webpage address you provide.
  • ✕ No subjective taste audits: We do not judge design artistry or brand tone.
  • ✕ Not a full audit: It is a helpful check of clarity and usability basics.
